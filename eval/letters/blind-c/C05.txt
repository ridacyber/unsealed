Your 36-month lease reaches its maturity date on Friday, December 18, 2026. By that date you need to do one of two things: turn the vehicle in, or buy it. This letter explains both, and what happens if you do neither.

IF YOU BUY THE VEHICLE

IF YOU DO NEITHER

The lease does not simply end. If the vehicle has not been returned or purchased by December 18, 2026, you will be charged a holdover payment equal to your monthly payment of $318.44 for each month, or part of a month, that you keep it. We may also treat the lease as in default and take the vehicle back at your expense.
