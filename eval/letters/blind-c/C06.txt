ANNUAL WAITING LIST CONFIRMATION - 30-FOOT SLIPS

Dear Mr. Marchetti-Rowe,

You are on the Port's waiting list for a permanent 30-foot slip in the Salterhead Boat Basin. As of October 1 you were number 41 on that list. Twelve slips of this size changed hands during the past year.

Once a year we ask everyone on the list to tell us whether they still want a slip. This keeps the list accurate and means that offers go to people who are ready to take them.

To stay on the list, you must confirm by Friday, November 6, 2026. You can do this in either of two ways:

  - sign and return the confirmation card enclosed with this letter, or
  - sign in to the Moorage Services page of the Port's website and choose the waiting list confirmation option.

There is no charge to confirm. The $80.00 waiting list deposit you paid when you joined the list in 2022 stays on file and will be credited to your first month's moorage when you accept a slip.

If you do not confirm by the date above, your name will be removed from the waiting list and your deposit will be forfeited. A person who has been removed and later wants a slip must send in a new application with a new deposit and starts again at the bottom of the list.

Please also check that the boat details and mailing address printed on the card are current, and correct anything that has changed.

Port policy on unconfirmed names. Under Moorage Rule 7.4, a name that is not confirmed during the annual review is placed in inactive status. It keeps its place in line and its deposit remains on file, but no slip is offered while the name is inactive. The holder may return to active status at any time by notifying the Harbor Office.
