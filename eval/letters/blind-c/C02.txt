NOTICE OF SPECIAL ASSESSMENT - ROOF REPLACEMENT, BUILDINGS A AND B

Dear Dr. Hartigan-Mele,

At its open meeting on September 29, 2026, the Board of Directors voted to replace the roofs on Buildings A and B and approved a special assessment to pay for the part of the work that the reserve fund cannot cover. This letter is your formal notice of that assessment.

How the amount was worked out

  Contract price for both roofs               $196,800.00
  Paid from the Association's reserve fund     $49,200.00
  Remainder to be assessed                    $147,600.00

The Declaration requires a special assessment to be divided equally among all 60 units. Each unit's share is therefore $2,460.00.

What you owe and when

The special assessment for Unit B-207 is $2,460.00. It is due in full on or before January 15, 2027.

This is a one-time charge. It is separate from your regular monthly dues of $285.00, which stay the same and remain payable on the first of each month as usual. The assessment will not be collected through your automatic dues payment. Please pay it by check payable to Thistlecombe Court Condominium Association, mailed or delivered to Stannick Community Management, or through the special assessment page of the owner portal. Write your unit number on the check.

There is no extra cost to pay by check or by electronic bank transfer on the owner portal. Owners who choose to pay by credit or debit card on the owner portal are charged a convenience fee of 2.95 percent of the payment. On the full assessment of $2,460.00, that fee is $72.57, so a card payment would come to $2,532.57. The convenience fee applies only to card payments and does not change your assessment, which is $2,460.00 however you pay.

If the assessment is not paid on time

An assessment that has not been paid in full by January 15, 2027 is delinquent. A late charge of $40.00 is added, the unpaid amount bears interest at 8 percent a year from the due date, and the Association may record a lien against the unit as the Declaration allows.

Work is scheduled to begin in March 2027, weather permitting. The contractor's schedule and the minutes of the September 29 meeting are posted on the owner portal.
