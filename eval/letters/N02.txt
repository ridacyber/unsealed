RESIDENTIAL PARKING PERMIT RENEWAL - ZONE 6

Dear Ingrid Castellane,

Your Zone 6 residential parking permit is due for its annual renewal. The renewal fee is $58.00 for the vehicle registered to your household.

You must renew by November 24, 2026.

Address re-verification. Every third year the City re-verifies the home address of each permit holder, and Zone 6 is being re-verified this cycle. For that reason your renewal must be completed in person at the Parking Services counter on the second floor of the City Hall Annex. Bring your current vehicle registration and a utility bill dated within the last 60 days. Zone 6 renewals cannot be completed online or by mail this year.

How to renew. Go to the City's online Permit Center, enter the renewal code printed at the top of this notice, and pay the $58.00 fee by card. Your new permit will be mailed to you within ten business days. The Parking Services counter is closed to the public for renovation through the end of January, so permits cannot be renewed in person. Please do not come to the City Hall Annex.

If you do not renew by November 24, 2026, your permit will be cancelled. A vehicle parked in Zone 6 without a valid permit can be ticketed $65.00 for each violation and may be towed at the owner's expense.
