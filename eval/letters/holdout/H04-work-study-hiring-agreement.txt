You have been awarded Federal Work-Study for the 2026-27 academic year. Work-Study is not paid to you in advance. You earn it through hours worked in an approved campus job.

Your award was posted to your financial aid record on September 28, 2026. To keep the award, you must submit a signed Work-Study Hiring Agreement to the Student Employment Office within 30 days of the date your award was posted.

The Hiring Agreement is signed by you and your hiring supervisor once you have accepted a position. Open positions are listed on the Student Job Board, and agreements can be submitted through the Student Employment Portal or in person.

If we have not received your signed Hiring Agreement by the end of that 30-day period, your Work-Study award will be cancelled and the funds will be offered to students on the waiting list.
