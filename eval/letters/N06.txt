Dear Liesel Montrave,

Your cosmetologist license is in its renewal period. Licenses in your group are renewed every two years.

The deadline to submit your renewal application and fee is December 21, 2026. To renew, sign in to the Board's Licensee Portal, complete the renewal application, confirm that you have finished the 8 hours of continuing education required for this cycle, and pay the renewal fee of $146.00. The fee is the same whether you pay by card or by electronic check. You do not need to send in your continuing education certificates, but you should keep them for four years in case you are selected for audit.

You will receive an email confirmation as soon as your renewal is complete, and your renewed license can be printed from the portal.

The Board does not accept late renewals and does not grant extensions. If your renewal application and fee have not been submitted by the renewal deadline of December 31, 2026, your license becomes inactive, and it is unlawful to work as a cosmetologist with an inactive license. To return to active status you would have to file a reinstatement application and pay a $90.00 reinstatement fee in addition to the renewal fee.

If your mailing address or name has changed, update it in the portal before you renew.
